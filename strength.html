STRENGTH IS A SKILL

Start light.
Learn well. Build steadily.

You do not need to lift the heaviest weight in the room to make meaningful progress.

Strength training can help develop muscular capability and support everyday activities. The most effective starting point is one that allows you to learn good technique, move with control, and recover between sessions.

Ask staff about equipment setup and gym guidelines. Begin with loads you can control comfortably, avoid rushing through repetitions, and stop if a movement causes pain or dizziness.

CONTROL BEFORE LOAD

THE BASICS MATTER
