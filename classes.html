VARIETY KEEPS IT INTERESTING

There is more than
one way to move.

Choose movement you can return to—not movement you feel you have to endure.

Cardio, resistance training, mobility, stretching, and mindful movement each bring something different to a fitness routine. The right mix depends on your goals, preferences, health, and available time.

The activities below are inspiration for your routine, not a confirmed class timetable. Please contact VIP GYM to verify current group classes, schedules, instructor availability, and booking requirements.

EXPLORE YOUR OPTIONS

Movement for every kind of energy.

01 / CARDIO

Find your pace

Use steady, comfortable cardiovascular movement to develop stamina over time. Begin at a pace that allows you to stay in control.

02 / POWER

Bring the energy

Power-based training can add variety and challenge. Learn technique first and scale intensity to suit your experience.

03 / MOBILITY

Make space to stretch

Gentle mobility and stretching can help you explore comfortable movement and transition into or out of training.

04 / CONDITIONING

Mix it up

Combine different movement patterns and effort levels to keep your routine engaging and well-rounded.
